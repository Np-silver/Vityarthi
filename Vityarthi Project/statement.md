Project Problem Statement

1. Problem Statement

Small community libraries and bookstores often struggle with manual record-keeping using paper ledgers or Excel sheets. This leads to errors in stock tracking, difficulty in finding books quickly, and miscalculations during checkout. There is a need for a lightweight, digital solution to automate these processes without the complexity of enterprise software.

2. Scope of the Project

The "BookKeeper" system is a desktop-based application focused on the core needs of a librarian. It covers the lifecycle of a book in the library: entry into the system, searching by users, and removal upon sale or loss. It does not cover user membership accounts or late fee calculations in this version.

3. Target Users

Librarians: To manage stock and organize the inventory.

Bookstore Clerks: To process sales and check prices quickly.

Administrators: To audit the total number of books available.

4. High-Level Features

Digital Cataloging: Converts physical inventory into a digital JSON database.

Instant Retrieval: Reduces search time from minutes to milliseconds.

Automated Stock Updates: Prevents selling/lending books that are out of stock.

Financial Accuracy: Ensures accurate price retrieval during checkout.
